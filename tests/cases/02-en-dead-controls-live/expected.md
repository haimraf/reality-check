# Reality Check - Shiftly (http://localhost:8765/page.html)
Basis: live page, interacted; not checked: mobile layout, anything past the first click of each control

## Verdict
Only after 3 fixes

The product is clear, but the only primary action does nothing and the live panel is static.

## Top 3 fixes
1. [verified] "Start free trial" goes nowhere (the URL gains a # and the page stays put) - the one primary action dies at the moment of intent - connect it to a working signup, or remove it until one exists.
2. [verified] The "LIVE ACROSS SHIFTLY" panel is static ("1,284 shifts filled today" and "312 swaps approved" were identical across two reloads) - a staged live widget makes the rest of the page look staged too - connect it to real data or drop the LIVE label and the pulsing dot.
3. [verified] "Pricing" in the nav returns 404 - visitors who check cost before a trial hit a dead end - point it at real pricing or take it out of the nav.

## Do not touch
- "Shift scheduling for restaurants" - a concrete noun and a concrete audience in four words
- The three "How it works" steps - a named process that shows how the product is used

## Why
Shiftly says clearly what it is and who it is for.
Every path that should prove it is real is dead or staged: the trial button, the live panel, and the pricing link.
