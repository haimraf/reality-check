# Reality Check - Shiftly (screenshot)
Basis: screenshot; not checked: whether "Start free trial", "Watch demo", the nav links, or the LIVE panel work

## Verdict
Only after 3 fixes

The product and the primary action are clear, but every piece of proof on the screen floats.

## Top 3 fixes
1. [observed] The "LIVE ACROSS SHIFTLY" numbers ("1,284 shifts filled today", "312 swaps approved") have no source on the page — they ask for trust the page does not earn — show where the numbers come from or drop the LIVE framing.
2. [observed] "Trusted by 10,000+ restaurants" stands alone, with no names or logos — an unsupported claim under the CTA reads as filler — connect it to evidence already on the page or tone it down.
3. [observed] The only testimonial is anonymous ("Restaurant owner") — an unnamed quote cannot carry trust — attach a name and restaurant or remove it.

## Do not touch
- "Shift scheduling for restaurants" — a concrete noun and a concrete audience
- The three "How it works" steps — a named process that shows how the product is used

## Why
On screen the page explains what Shiftly is and what to do next.
The proof around it is unsupported, and nothing in this screenshot proves that it is fake or that any button is broken.
